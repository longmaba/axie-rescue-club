# Two Ways Home — three fresh-player sessions

Status: **prepared; no human playtests recorded yet**. Automated browser runs verify functionality and rendering. They do not establish whether new players understand or enjoy the puzzle.

Invite three people who have not watched a playthrough. Use the same current build, a clean browser profile for each participant, and their normal input device. Keep a note of build, device/browser, lesson selection and any technical problem. No wallet or account is needed.

## Give this prompt

“A traveler is stranded across the brook. Bring them back to camp. You can explore and try ideas; take your time.”

Let the person start, choose a field lesson if they notice it, and explore. Do not name the solution or choose a companion for them. The optional in-game hint is available. If they ask for outside help, record that before assisting. Stop if they want to stop; do not turn the session into a test of the person.

## Observe the first expedition

- Time until first movement, inspection and successful interaction.
- Whether inspecting an object is understood as distinct from using an ability.
- First route attempted; what clue led to that choice.
- First discovery of a combination: rolling then rooting, or growing then launching/splashing.
- Wrong experiments, hint use, confusion, and recovery without restarting.
- Whether the returning traveler visibly boards and reaches home.
- Whether the person notices their Axies' individual contributions on the postcard.

After they complete or stop, ask: “What made your solution work?”, “What else could you try?”, and “Did your field lesson change anything?” Then let them explore another route if they want to. Record this as prompted replay, separately from the first unprompted attempt.

## What to change from the findings

- If two people mistake inspection for automatic interaction, improve the contextual Try affordance and first inspection feedback.
- If two people need an outside explanation to discover a combination, strengthen the object's visible clue before adding more text.
- If a person becomes stranded or loses the traveler, treat it as a gameplay defect and record the precise steps.
- If two people cannot explain a second possibility after finishing, improve the alternate route's visibility and postcard invitation.
- If the field lesson is never noticed or understood, revise its explanation and visible consequence.

Aim for a discoverable combination and a complete first expedition in roughly three to five minutes, then measure what actually happens. These three sessions provide qualitative direction; they are not retention or population difficulty statistics.
